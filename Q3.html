Contrasting Tim Berners Lee's Original Research to The Current World Wide Web

Tim Berners Original Research

I mentioned earlier in my response to question one that Tim accomplished most of his goals while working at CERN. Now, CERN is a nuclear research organization with one of the largest particle physics laboratories. Tim didn't create the World Wide Web for general purposes. He designed it for a specific purpose: to share important documents and research notes internally at CERN between compatible and incompatible computers.

Comparing it to The World Wide Web

So, how does the World Wide Web now compare to Tim's original purpose for his research? The World Wide Web is used in a vast majority of ways and solves problems beyond file sharing. Though it also brings problems with it. Now we use it for Social media, e-commerce, video games, and more. The scope is much bigger than what Tim intended, but at its core, it's still the same. Data streams are transferred from computer to computer. Also, we still use HTML today!
